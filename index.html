How to play

Each pearl gets one trip through the midway. Lit pegs stay solid until the shot ends, then disappear to open up your next shot.

  1. Light every sun

    Line up the dotted guide and fire. Clear all the orange suns before your pearls run out.

  2. Set off a chain

    Light several pegs in one shot. A green diamond triggers your performer’s signature power.

  3. Catch a second chance

    Land in the moving basket to earn a pearl back. Score 2,500 points in a single shot for another.

Controls & accessibility

Mouse Move to aim, hold Shift to fine-tune and click to fire. Keyboard Use the arrow keys to aim, Shift for fine adjustments and Space to fire. Press I to read your aim and F to follow a shot at double speed. Touch Drag to aim, then tap Fire. Hold still and slide sideways for fine aim.

Escape pauses the game. M toggles sound. Open the gear for graphics quality, high-contrast aim, reduced motion and separate music and effects volumes. Adventure saves after each settled shot. Select the score during play to see where your points came from.
