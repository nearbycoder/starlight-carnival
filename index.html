How to play

Every bounce
counts.

  1. Light every sun

    Line up the dotted guide and fire. Clear all the orange suns before your pearls run out.

  2. Set off a chain

    Light several pegs in one shot. A green diamond triggers your performer’s signature power.

  3. Catch a second chance

    Land in the moving basket to earn a pearl back. Score 2,500 points in a single shot for another.

Controls & accessibility

Mouse Move to aim and click to fire. Keyboard Use the arrow keys to aim, Shift for fine adjustments and Space to fire. Touch Drag to aim, then tap Fire.

Escape pauses the game. Open the gear for graphics quality, high-contrast aim, reduced motion and separate music and effects volumes. Campaign progress and records save in this browser.
